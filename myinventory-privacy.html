Privacy Policy for MyInventory

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

MyInventory is built and maintained by Adrian Rizeanu as a free application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

MyInventory is designed to function primarily locally on your device. The app stores item data, stock levels, and pricing information directly on your local device storage. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data or inventory details to external servers or third-party databases.

2. Device Permissions

Depending on the features enabled, MyInventory may request certain device permissions (such as local storage access or camera access for scanning barcodes/taking product photos). These permissions are used strictly to enable app functionality on your device and are never used to harvest private data.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not incorporate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or external advertising networks that collect identifiable user information.

4. Children's Privacy

MyInventory does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
